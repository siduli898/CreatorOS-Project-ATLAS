# Project ATLAS Trust & Safety SOP

CreatorOS is designed for adults only and requires adult age assurance before protected access. Performer records and consent documentation are maintained as restricted compliance data.

## Prohibited
CSAM/CSAE, non-consensual intimate imagery, trafficking/coercion, real sexual violence, bestiality, necrophilia, and physical-service solicitation are prohibited and require immediate safety escalation.

## Moderation
Uploads should enter quarantine and automated safety screening before publication. High-severity reports bypass ordinary queues and enter emergency legal/safety workflows.
